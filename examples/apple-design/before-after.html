Hierarchy

Six equal toolbar buttons became one blue primary action plus one tinted secondary; stats moved from a bordered strip into four spaced summary cards with 22px figures.

Navigation

Underlined links on a dark block became a translucent sidebar with icons, a clear selected state, and an opaque fallback when transparency is reduced.

Contrast

Grey-on-grey bordered table rows became white rounded rows with soft hairlines and color-coded status pills (blue / amber / green) that stay readable at 14px.
